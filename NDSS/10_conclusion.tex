%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We presented an end-to-end framework that transforms natural-language Web PKI
normative text into auditable lint code. On RFC~5280 and CABF~BR, the
pipeline recalled 2,080 candidate records and retained 1,567 as normative rules through
its adjudication procedure.
Under our lintability criterion, 260, or 16.6\%, are lintable.
Existing zlint lints fully cover 165, leaving 95 uncovered normative rules for generation.
The restricted generator produced 76 compilable lints with at
least one GENERIC atom, 64 of which passed the unanimous code--normative-rule
semantic-equivalence gate. Together with 15 NON\_GENERIC-only lints, the final accepted
lint set contained 79 lints, which were added to zlint. Certificate detection confirmed five
finding types across the evaluated corpora, including findings not flagged by
native zlint. Within the evaluated Web PKI normative sources and confirmation protocol, these
results show that source-linked normative-rule extraction, explicit lintability
determination, and restricted generation can expose actionable gaps in static
certificate-lint coverage.
