%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We presented an end-to-end framework that transforms natural-language Web PKI
normative text into auditable static lint code. On RFC~5280 and CABF~BR, the
pipeline recalled 2,080 candidates and identified 1,567 genuine normative rules.
Under our single-certificate static-check criterion, 260 (16.6\%) are lintable.
Existing zlint lints fully cover 165, leaving 95 uncovered rules for generation.
The restricted generator produced 93 compilable lints, including 77 with at
least one GENERIC atom. Of these, 79 passed the unanimous code--specification
synonymy gate and were added to zlint. Certificate detection confirmed five
finding types, including four de-duplicated external instances not flagged by
native zlint. Within the evaluated standards and confirmation protocol, these
results show that source-linked rule extraction, explicit lintability
classification, and restricted generation can expose actionable gaps in static
certificate-lint coverage.
