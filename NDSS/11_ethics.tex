\section*{Ethics Considerations}

We strictly follow the Menlo Report~\cite{kenneally2012menlo} and established best
practices for network measurement research~\cite{partridge2016ethical,allman2007issues}.
All data analyzed in this work is publicly available: public normative documents,
zlint's open-source testdata, the chains served by the Tranco Top~1M domains, and a
sample of public CT logs. The only active component is the
one-time TLS-handshake collection of the Tranco chains, where we strictly limit the
scan rate to minimize the impact on real networks and production services and perform
only the handshake needed to retrieve the served chain. All remaining analysis is
offline, and the work involves no user traffic, no personal data, and no human
subjects.
